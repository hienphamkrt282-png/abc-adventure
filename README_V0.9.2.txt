ABC Adventure Version 0.9.2

New: after a correct word, the success dialog displays the English story, an IPA transcription, and a button to replay the whole paragraph. The story is read aloud automatically after the word; use Listen to the story again to replay it.

IPA is generated from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (General American pronunciation); bracketed words indicate a word not found in that dictionary. Browser speech quality depends on the English voice installed on the device.
